# Deskripsi Konten Video Portofolio

## 1. Platform freelance (English)

**Title:** AI CCTV Suspicious Activity Detection System with Web Dashboard

Regular CCTV only records. Most theft is found the next day, when the footage is checked.

I built a system that watches the camera for you. It detects people and motorbikes, follows each person on screen, and sends an alert with a photo the moment someone acts suspiciously.

**What it does**
- Works with a webcam, an IP CCTV camera (RTSP), or recorded video
- Picks the right mode by itself: general area or parking lot
- Parking rules: lingering or crouching by a motorbike, pushing a bike away, hiding behind a bike
- Ignores people who are just walking by
- Restricted zones with guard hours (for example 10 PM to 6 AM)
- Instant alerts with a saved photo and a full alert history
- Staff mark each alert as real or false alarm with one tap
- Mobile-friendly dashboard with secure login and session control
- Protection against brute force, bots, CSRF, and SQL injection (passed all 29 of my attack tests)

**Tech stack:** Python, YOLO26s (retrained), BoT-SORT with ReID, Flask (3-tier architecture), MySQL

**Available two ways**
1. **Buy this system.** I install it, set it up for your cameras, and show you how to use it.
2. **Order a custom system.** Need different rules, other objects, or a completely different app? I build web apps, dashboards, and AI vision systems for your needs.

Message me to discuss your project.

**Judul:** Sistem CCTV AI Pendeteksi Aktivitas Mencurigakan dengan Dashboard Web

CCTV biasa hanya merekam. Pencurian biasanya baru ketahuan besoknya, saat rekaman diperiksa.

Saya membuat sistem yang mengawasi kamera untuk Anda. Sistem mengenali orang dan sepeda motor, mengikuti setiap orang di layar, dan langsung mengirim peringatan beserta foto saat ada gerak-gerik mencurigakan.

- Memilih mode sendiri: area umum atau parkiran
- Aturan parkiran: berlama-lama atau jongkok di dekat motor, motor didorong pergi, bersembunyi di balik motor
- Orang yang hanya lewat tidak ditandai
- Zona terlarang dengan jam jaga (misalnya pukul 22.00 sampai 06.00)
- Peringatan langsung dengan foto tersimpan dan riwayat lengkap
- Petugas menilai setiap peringatan: benar atau alarm palsu, cukup satu ketukan
- Dashboard nyaman di HP, dengan login dan sesi yang aman
- Terlindung dari tebak sandi, bot, CSRF, dan SQL injection (lolos 29 dari 29 uji serangan)

**Teknologi:** Python, YOLO26s (dilatih ulang), BoT-SORT dengan ReID, Flask (arsitektur tiga lapis), MySQL
